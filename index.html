Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you get here if my boiler breaks down?

We prioritise no-heating and no-hot-water calls and usually attend within 24 hours in Stoke and the surrounding areas.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

We give you the price for diagnosis before we come out, and it's put towards the repair if you go ahead. No surprises on the day.

Can you work around tenants?

Yes. We arrange appointments directly with tenants at times that suit them, and send landlords the service paperwork afterwards.

How long does a bathroom installation take?

Most standard bathroom replacements take one to two weeks depending on tiling. We give you a firm schedule before we start.

Do you service all boiler makes?

Yes, we work on all major gas boiler brands and can source parts quickly for common models.

Do you give a written quote before starting work?

Always. You get a written quote with the full scope and price, and nothing starts until you've agreed it.
